Project Overview

Tracking Geopolitical Agreements & Strategic Supply Chains

This platform tracks global bilateral partnerships, multilateral frameworks, and funding commitments securing critical mineral supply chains for clean energy and defense infrastructure. Such agreements have gained popularity in the last few years, especially in response to Chinese export restrictions and US tariffs.

Since President Trump retook office in January 2025, Washington has pursued an aggressive “all of government” approach to critical minerals (CM), through executive and legislative efforts. This includes bilateral and plurilateral deals with key partners, laws and executive orders. No longer just a climate or supply chain question, Washington now treats CM policy as an essential security, economic and geopolitical driver.
